# Leave and working-hours policy (synthetic)

## Working day
A standard working day is 8 hours, starting at 09:00. Planning tools must not assign more hours to a person than their available hours for the day.

## Half-day leave
Half-day leave means 4 working hours, taken from 09:00 to 13:00 unless agreed otherwise. A person on half-day leave keeps their normal responsibilities for those hours.

## Task caps during leave
During partial leave a project manager may cap the number of time-boxed tasks given to a person, for example "at most one of" a set of tasks. Such caps must be recorded with the plan and reviewed by the project manager before tickets are created.
